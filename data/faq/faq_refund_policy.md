# Refunds and duplicate charges

If you were charged more than once for the same upgrade attempt, this is usually
caused by a payment retry after a failed authorization — the failed attempt's
hold can still show as a pending charge on your bank statement for a few hours
even though it was never actually captured.

If multiple charges were genuinely captured (not just pending holds), each extra
charge is eligible for a full refund. Refunds typically post back to the original
payment method within 5-10 business days depending on the bank.

Refunds above $50 or affecting more than one charge require manual review before
being issued — they are not processed automatically.
