# Plans and upgrading

Free plan accounts can upgrade to Pro at any time from Settings > Billing. Pro
unlocks export features, higher usage limits, and priority support.

If an upgrade payment fails, your account stays on the Free plan and no charge
should be captured. If you retried the upgrade after a failure, see the refund
policy FAQ for how duplicate charges from retries are handled.

Downgrading from Pro to Free takes effect at the end of the current billing
cycle, not immediately.
