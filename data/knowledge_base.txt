# faq_account_access

If you cannot log in, first confirm the issue isn't a service-wide outage (check
the outage status topic) before troubleshooting your own account.

Password resets are self-service via the "Forgot password" link on the login
page. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

Enterprise accounts with SSO configured should contact their organization's
admin for access issues rather than using the standard password reset flow.

# faq_billing_plans

Free plan accounts can upgrade to Pro at any time from Settings > Billing. Pro
unlocks export features, higher usage limits, and priority support.

If an upgrade payment fails, your account stays on the Free plan and no charge
should be captured. If you retried the upgrade after a failure, see the refund
policy topic for how duplicate charges from retries are handled.

Downgrading from Pro to Free takes effect at the end of the current billing
cycle, not immediately.

# faq_dark_mode

Dark mode is currently in limited rollout. Go to Settings > Appearance to choose
between Light and System Default. A dedicated Dark toggle is coming in a future
release — there is no way to force dark mode independently of your OS theme yet.

Known issue: on some Mac setups, selecting "System Default" does not immediately
pick up a dark OS theme. Toggling back to Light and then to System Default again
usually fixes it. A permanent fix is being tracked internally.

Scheduled theme switching (e.g. auto-dark at a specific time) is not supported.

# faq_data_export

Pro plan accounts can export data as CSV or JSON from Settings > Export. Exports
are generated asynchronously and emailed as a download link once ready, usually
within a few minutes for most account sizes.

Free plan accounts do not have access to export features. Upgrading to Pro
unlocks export immediately, with no separate activation step required.

# faq_outage_status

Current system status is published at status.company.com. Note that the status
page is updated by an on-call engineer and can lag behind reality by several
minutes during a fast-moving incident — if you are seeing errors but the page
says "all systems operational," the page may simply not have been updated yet.

A 500 error on load typically indicates a backend service issue rather than a
problem with your account or browser. Trying multiple browsers or devices and
seeing the same result is a strong signal the issue is server-side, not local.

Enterprise customers with an active incident affecting multiple seats should be
escalated for a paged response rather than a standard support reply.

# faq_refund_policy

If you were charged more than once for the same upgrade attempt, this is usually
caused by a payment retry after a failed authorization — the failed attempt's
hold can still show as a pending charge on your bank statement for a few hours
even though it was never actually captured.

If multiple charges were genuinely captured (not just pending holds), each extra
charge is eligible for a full refund. Refunds typically post back to the original
payment method within 5-10 business days depending on the bank.

Refunds above $50 or affecting more than one charge require manual review before
being issued — they are not processed automatically.

# faq_notification_settings

Email and in-app notifications can be toggled independently from Settings >
Notifications — turning off email digests does not disable in-app alerts, and
vice versa.

Weekly summary emails go out every Monday at 9am in the account's configured
timezone. Changing your timezone takes effect on the following Monday, not
immediately, since that week's digest may already be queued.

Enterprise admins can set a notification policy that applies to every seat on
the account; individual members can only loosen it (e.g. add channels), not
turn off notifications their admin has marked mandatory.

# faq_team_seat_management

Enterprise and Pro-team accounts manage seats from Settings > Team. Adding a
seat mid-cycle is prorated for the remainder of the current billing period;
removing a seat does not refund the current period but stops billing for it
starting next cycle.

An invited member has 7 days to accept before the invite expires and the seat
is released back to the available pool. Re-inviting the same email after
expiry sends a fresh invite and consumes a seat again only once they accept.

Transferring a seat from one person to another (e.g. an employee leaving) does
not require removing and re-adding — use "Reassign seat" on the team member's
row to keep their historical data attributed to the seat, not the person.

# faq_mobile_app_login

The mobile app uses the same credentials as the web app, but sessions are
separate — logging out on web does not log you out on mobile, and vice versa.

If a one-time code never arrives on mobile, check that notifications for the
app aren't silenced at the OS level first; the code is also always visible by
pulling down to refresh the login screen, not just via push notification.

SSO-enabled enterprise accounts must complete the SSO login through the
in-app browser the first time; after that, the mobile app can use a cached
session without re-prompting SSO on every open.
